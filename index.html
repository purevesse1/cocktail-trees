      About Cocktail Trees

      Gin+ sweet vermouthGin & It+ CampariNegroni

      A personal cocktail recipe collection, organised in tree form. One radial tree per base spirit, branching outward by added ingredients.

      Reading a tree

      Arrows
      Each one lists what to add (+) or drop (−) compared with the drink it starts from. Follow them from the centre and you have the recipe.
      Black and white
      A black box means your bar has everything for that drink. Tick what you own under My bar and the boxes update.
      Recipes
      Tap a drink to open its card with quantities and method. The neighbours move aside and drift back when you close it.
